# Perfume colour and form revision

The client favours expressive red, gold and ornate detail. Each bottle now uses its own shape and note-led palette while retaining gold as the family connection.

| Fragrance | Colour and form | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Noir Sultana | Garnet glass, stepped shoulders, engraved gold cap | Dark rose, smoked oud and amber; the dramatic house signature |
| Oud Royale | Smoked umber facets, dark wood cap, antique gold | Oud wood, leather and saffron; depth and texture |
| Rose Sultana | Rose-ruby fluted oval glass, sculpted gold rose cap | Rose and raspberry; a floral identity rather than a pink recolour |
| Amber d’Or | Honey-gold fan-shaped glass, curved gold cap | Amber, vanilla and tonka; warm light and softness |

Product facts/prices were preserved. These remain packaging concepts, not verified final products. Card accents echo the fragrance family and the existing note text supports that meaning. The duplicate homepage house band was removed and the hero-bottom wording simplified.
