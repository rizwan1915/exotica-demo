Which fragrance should I explore?

For woods, explore Oud Royale with Cambodian oud, saffron and leather. Rose Sultana pairs Taif rose with raspberry and musk. Amber d’Or brings golden amber, vanilla and tonka. Noir Sultana is the house signature, with rose, smoked oud and golden amber.

What format is Noir Sultana?

Noir Sultana is presented here as a 100 ML eau de parfum at Rs 14,500. The bottles shown are design concepts; final packaging may differ.
