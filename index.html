Our Story

Born of Silk Roads

House of Vivian Exotica began with a single conviction — that a fragrance is not worn, it is inherited. Drawing upon centuries of oriental perfumery, our atelier blends rare resins, hand-picked roses and aged ouds into compositions worthy of a maharaja's court.

Every flacon is filled by hand in small batches, sealed in crimson wax and finished with 24k gilding. We do not chase seasons. We craft legacies.
